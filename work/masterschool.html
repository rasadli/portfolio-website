MasterSchool Portal

One system for running a training centre.

Open portal ↗ (opens in a new tab)

01 Project thesis

A training centre has many records that depend on each other: students, groups, contracts, payments, salaries and attendance. The portal brings them into one place, so reporting does not depend on stitching separate tools together.

02 Engineering context

Role

Stack
